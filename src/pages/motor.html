How this works

Third Party Premium = Base TP rate + Extra Seat Loading (seats above 5) + Extra TPPDL loading (limits above GHC 8,000).

Own Damage = Sum Insured × rate (rate depends on cover type and ICE/EV). Excess Buy Back (only for 100% cover) = 10% of Own Damage for private vehicles, 15% for commercial vehicles.

  • Third Party — Third Party Premium only
  • Third Party Amplified — Third Party Premium + a fixed loading
  • Fire & Theft / Comprehensive — Own Damage + Third Party Premium (90% cover), or Own Damage + Excess Buy Back + Third Party Premium (100% cover)
